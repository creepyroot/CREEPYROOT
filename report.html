WHAT THE EVIDENCE SUGGESTS

The editorial opportunity.

Four connected content pillars

Leadership and character; practical success and execution; career readiness and independent thinking; programs and events. This is a proposed organizing framework inferred from the sample, rather than a claim about the client’s original strategy.

One idea, several expressions

For portfolio presentation, connect each approved long-form video to its script, short clips, post creative and brochure or campaign. This would show the studio’s contribution more clearly than a total of undifferentiated posts.

RESEARCH METHOD & LIMITATIONS

What can be established.

Public search covered official channels, indexed LinkedIn articles and posts, YouTube guest appearances and podcast distributor listings. Exact displayed dates were preferred; crawl dates, relative ages and event dates were not treated as publication dates. The same guest conversation is counted once across video and audio. No platform login, private analytics or full account export was available.

  • Public search indexing is incomplete. Missing months and channels cannot establish inactivity.
  • The selected research period comes from the studio’s choice of “last 12 months.” It is not independent proof of contract dates.
  • Brochures, scripts, raw footage and marketing plans were described by the studio but not supplied for inspection. No speculative samples are presented as client deliverables.
  • Public viewer and follower counts are excluded from campaign results. They lack a reliable baseline and production attribution.
  • To finish a complete annual inventory: supply account exports or a content calendar with permalinks, dates, format, parent asset, studio role, approved portfolio use and analytics at start/end of the period.
